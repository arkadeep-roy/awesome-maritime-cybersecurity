# GMDSS

The Global Maritime Distress and Safety System supports distress alerting and maritime safety communications.

## Security relevance

GMDSS intersects with safety-of-life systems. Research must be handled carefully. Security discussion should focus on dependency mapping, resilience, training, monitoring, and the impact of GNSS interference on systems that depend on position and time.

## Boundary

No distress-signal testing, spoofing, or operational radio procedures are accepted in this repository.
