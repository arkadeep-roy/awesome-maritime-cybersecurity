# Port and Terminal Systems

Port environments combine enterprise IT, terminal operating systems, gate systems, crane and yard operations, customs integrations, EDI/API workflows, and third-party logistics platforms.

## Security themes

- Availability of terminal operating systems
- Identity and access management across contractors and shipping lines
- API and EDI integrity
- Ransomware resilience
- Segmentation of OT, business systems, and public-facing portals
- Backup and manual fallback procedures
