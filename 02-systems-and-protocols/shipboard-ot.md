# Shipboard OT

Shipboard OT includes propulsion, power management, machinery control, ballast, cargo systems, dynamic positioning, and monitoring systems.

## Security themes

- Segmentation between crew IT, business IT, navigation, and control systems
- Secure remote maintenance
- Patch and backup constraints
- Vendor access governance
- Asset inventory and firmware versioning
- Incident response under bandwidth and availability constraints
