# GNSS and GPS

GNSS provides positioning and timing to navigation systems. Maritime operations often combine GNSS with ECDIS, AIS, radar, and manual navigation checks.

## Security relevance

GNSS jamming and spoofing can affect perceived vessel position. Recent public reporting and research describe large-scale maritime GNSS interference and AIS-derived detection methods.

## Safe research paths

- Analyze historical AIS/GNSS anomaly datasets.
- Study detection methods such as kinematic filtering and multi-vessel clustering.
- Use shielded, authorized labs for receiver testing.
- Compare GNSS with radar, visual bearings, inertial systems, and route context.
