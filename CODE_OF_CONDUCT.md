# Code of Conduct

This project follows a professional research standard. Contributors are expected to discuss maritime cybersecurity with care because the domain includes safety-critical systems, critical infrastructure, commercial operations, and public-interest research.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and precise in technical disagreement.
- Review sources before challenging or accepting claims.
- Separate confirmed facts from reported, suspected, or contested information.
- Treat safety-critical topics with appropriate caution.
- Respect coordinated disclosure and program scope boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- Encouraging unauthorized access or disruption
- Publishing target lists of real maritime infrastructure
- Pressuring contributors to disclose private program details
- Misrepresenting uncertain claims as confirmed facts

## Enforcement

Project reviewers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that reduce source quality, create legal or safety risk, or undermine technical credibility.
