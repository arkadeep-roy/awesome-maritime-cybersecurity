# Industry Guidelines on Cyber Security On Board Ships, Version 5

The Industry Guidelines on Cyber Security On Board Ships are one of the most useful practical references for shipboard cyber-risk management.

## Current version

Version 5 of the Industry Guidelines on Cyber Security On Board Ships was published on 14 November 2024.

Sources:

- [intercargo.org](https://www.intercargo.org/publications/guidelines-cyber-security-onboard-ships/)
- [maritimeglobalsecurity.org](https://www.maritimeglobalsecurity.org/media/g3qlxdaw/2024-11-14-guidelines_on_cyber_security-v5-final.pdf)
- [bimco.org](https://www.bimco.org/regulatory-affairs/policy-positions/cyber-risk-management/)

## What it covers

The guidelines are intended to help shipping organizations manage cyber risk onboard ships. They are relevant to:

- shipboard cyber-risk management
- crew awareness
- system inventory
- access control
- third-party and remote access
- incident response
- recovery
- shipboard IT and OT
- safe operations

## Why this belongs here ?

For offensive researchers, the guidelines help translate a technical finding into maritime operational impact. For defenders, they provide a practical baseline for hardening and governance.

Examples:

- An exposed remote-support interface is not just a web finding; it can become a third-party access-control issue.
- A missing asset inventory can weaken cyber-risk management and incident response.
- Assess USB-based chart or software update workflows as part of onboard cyber risk.
