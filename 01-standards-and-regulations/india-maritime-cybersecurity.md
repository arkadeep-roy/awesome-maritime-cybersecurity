# India Maritime Cybersecurity

India deserves a dedicated maritime cybersecurity section because its ports, shipping, coastal security, inland waterways, Indian Ocean Region posture, and critical-information-infrastructure policy are usually underrepresented in global maritime cyber resources.

## Why India matters

India has a large coastline, major and non-major ports, strategic sea lanes, and a rapidly digitalizing port and shipping ecosystem. For security researchers, this creates a useful research angle that is not already saturated by the usual Western references around IMO, USCG, ENISA, and IACS.

Research angles:

- Indian-flagged ship cyber-risk guidance
- Indian Register of Shipping cyber safety guidance
- Port cybersecurity and critical infrastructure
- Indian Ocean Region threat context
- Training and workforce development
- Port and logistics incident history
- Research on Indian port risk

## Directorate General of Shipping cyber-risk circular

The Directorate General of Shipping issued Engineering Circular No. 06 of 2017 on implementation of cybersecurity risk mitigation measures onboard Indian-flagged ships. IRClass summarizes that cyber risks are required to be addressed in the ship safety management system.

Sources:

- [irclass.org](https://www.irclass.org/technical-circulars/dg-shipping-circular-reg-implementation-of-cyber-security-risk-mitigation-measures-onboard-indian-flag-ships/)
- [dgma.gov.in](https://dgma.gov.in/engineering-wing/ew-circulars/engineering-ism-circulars)

Relevance:

- Compares India’s approach with IMO MSC.428(98).
- Maps shipboard cyber-risk management to safety management systems.
- Adds India-specific context to maritime cyber compliance learning.

## Indian Register of Shipping / IRClass

The correct name to use is Indian Register of Shipping, commonly branded as IRClass. IRClass has published maritime cyber-safety guidance, including Guidelines on Maritime Cyber Safety, Revision 1, March 2024.

Sources:

- [irclass.org](https://www.irclass.org/marine/cyber-risk-management/)
- [irclass.org](https://www.irclass.org/media/7145/irs-g-saf-02_guidelines-on-maritime-cyber-safety-rev1_march-2024.pdf)

How to classify it in this repo:

```text
Category: classification society / Indian maritime assurance
Scope: ships, cyber-risk management, maritime cyber safety guidance
```

## Bureau of Port Security

India has moved to establish the Bureau of Port Security as a statutory body under the Merchant Shipping Act, 2025. Public government material says the Bureau will be responsible for regulatory and oversight functions relating to ship and port-facility security and explicitly mentions cybersecurity and port IT infrastructure protection.

Source:

- [pib.gov.in](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2206443)

Relevance:

- Tracks Indian port-security modernization.
- Shows how cybersecurity is being integrated into port-facility security.
- Provides India-specific context for standards and regulatory coverage.

## C3iHub and Indian Ports Association training

C3iHub at IIT Kanpur conducted specialized maritime cybersecurity training for Indian Ports Association officials in December 2025. The training reportedly covered compliance, regulatory requirements, OSINT, dark web analysis, digital forensics and incident response, network assessment, and cloud security.

Sources:

- [iitk.ac.in](https://www.iitk.ac.in/specialized-training-on-maritime-cybersecurity-for-ipa)
- [iitk.ac.in](https://www.iitk.ac.in/media-coverage-training-on-maritime-cybersecurity-for-ipa)

Relevance:

- Shows Indian maritime cyber capacity-building beyond policy documents.
- Relevant for training, workforce development, and institutional ecosystem mapping.
