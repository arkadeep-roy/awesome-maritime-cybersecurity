## MaCySTe

Labels: `lab`, `offensive-research`, `defensive`

Maritime Cyber Security Testbed.

- [Repository](https://github.com/CRACK-MCR/MaCySTe)
- [Paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S235271102300122X)

Maritime use cases:

- Shipboard cyber-physical simulation.
