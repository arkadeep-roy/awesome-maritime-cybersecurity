# Autonomous Vessels, AI, and Maritime Cybersecurity

Autonomous maritime systems expand the cyber problem beyond traditional IT and OT. They introduce AI perception, sensor fusion, autonomy stacks, remote-control centers, and cyber-physical decision-making.

## Preventing Adversarial AI Attacks Against Autonomous Situational Awareness

This 2025 paper proposes Data Fusion Cyber Resilience for maritime autonomous systems and evaluates adversarial AI attacks against maritime situational awareness.

Source:

- [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21609)

Operational relevance:

- Shows adversarial AI is not only an image-classification issue.
- Connects AI attacks to maritime situational awareness.
- Relevant for autonomous vessels, remote operations, and AI-enabled bridge systems.

## A Offensive Researching Framework for Securing AI in Maritime Autonomous Systems

This paper proposes a offensive-researching framework for AI systems in maritime autonomous systems and demonstrates vulnerabilities including poisoning and adversarial patch attacks.

Source:

- [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.11500)

Operational relevance:

- Relevant to dual-use research.
- Shows how offensive-research methodology can be applied to autonomous maritime AI.
- Relevant for secure-by-design and post-deployment evaluation.

## A Survey of Cyber Security for Maritime Autonomous Surface Ships

This 2025 survey analyzes cyber-security research trends for Maritime Autonomous Surface Ships.

Sources:

- [trid.trb.org](https://trid.trb.org/View/2561813)
- [researchgate.net](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/391170769_A_Survey_of_Cyber_Security_for_Maritime_Autonomous_Surface_Ships_Opportunities_Challenges_and_Future_Directions)

Operational relevance:

- Helps separate traditional vessel cybersecurity from MASS-specific cybersecurity.
- Relevant to remote-control centers, autonomy levels, and cyber-physical security.

## Related research directions

- Sensor-fusion security
- Adversarial AI against camera/radar perception
- GNSS spoofing against autonomous navigation
- Remote-control center security
- Safe fallback behavior
- Human oversight and alert fatigue
- Simulation environments for AI red teaming
