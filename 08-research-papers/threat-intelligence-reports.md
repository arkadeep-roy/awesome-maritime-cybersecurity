# Maritime Threat Intelligence Reports

Maritime cybersecurity threat-intelligence reports are listed here as attributed datasets, not neutral global incident totals.

## CYTUR 2026 Maritime Cyber Threat White Paper

CYTUR's 2026 Maritime Cyber Threat White Paper is the source behind widely repeated figures saying maritime cyber incidents rose by 103% in 2025. Industry coverage reports 828 maritime cyber incidents in 2025, compared with 408 in 2024.

Sources:

- [cytur.net](https://cytur.net/en/resources-media-reports/)
- [industrialcyber.co](https://industrialcyber.co/reports/maritime-cyber-incidents-jump-103-as-cytur-warns-smart-ships-under-fire-urges-secure-by-design-overhaul/)
- [safety4sea.com](https://safety4sea.com/maritime-cyber-incidents-jumped-103-in-2025/)

Repo note:

Use this as vendor threat intelligence. Do not present the numbers as an official global census.

## Cydome Maritime Cybersecurity Trends Report 2026

Cydome's 2026 report covers AI-driven phishing, identity fraud, GPS spoofing, edge-device exposure, OT security, and business impact. Cydome reports a 150% increase in maritime OT cyber incidents and an 800% increase in attacks on routers, firewalls, VPNs, and other edge devices.

Sources:

- [cydome.io](https://cydome.io/cydome-publishes-its-annual-maritime-cybersecurity-trends-report-for-2026/)
- [cydome.io](https://cydome.io/cydome-maritime-trends-report-2026/)
- [industrialcyber.co](https://industrialcyber.co/download/maritime-cybersecurity-in-2026-ai-driven-threats-ot-exposure-and-the-new-operational-risk-landscape-cydome/)

Repo note:

Relevant for current threat themes, especially edge-device exposure and OT risk. Keep all statistics attributed to Cydome.

## DNV Maritime Cyber Priority 2024/25

DNV's Maritime Cyber Priority 2024/25 report is relevant because it is based on survey data from maritime professionals. DNV reports that about 31% of maritime professionals reported at least one cyberattack in the 12 months leading up to October 2024.

Sources:

- [dnv.com](https://www.dnv.com/cyber/insights/publications/maritime-cyber-priority-2024/)
- [dnv.com](https://www.dnv.com/expert-story/maritime-impact/tackling-a-growing-cybersecurity-threat-in-an-increasingly-connected-industry/)

Repo note:

Use this to show industry perception and organizational maturity, not just technical vulnerabilities.

## MCAD: Maritime Cyber Attack Database

MCAD is a public incident database developed by NHL Stenden University of Applied Sciences. It contains incidents dating back to 2001 and reports more than 160 maritime cyber incidents.

Sources:

- [nhlstenden.com](https://www.nhlstenden.com/en/maritime-cyber-attack-database)
- [maritimecybersecurity.nl](https://maritimecybersecurity.nl/info)

Repo note:

Use MCAD to cross-check incident history and avoid overclaiming from single-vendor reports.
