## Are Indian ports safe?

The 2024 paper "Are Indian ports safe? Identifying, analysing and prioritizing risks associated with Indian ports" develops a framework for identifying and ranking risks across 12 major Indian ports.

## Indian Ocean Region cybersecurity context

The Indian Ocean Region is strategically important and includes major shipping lanes, ports, energy routes, and geopolitical competition. Treat cybersecurity alongside maritime security, port modernization, and critical infrastructure resilience.

Current research gaps include India-specific maritime cyber incidents, port community systems, Indian Ocean chokepoints, GNSS interference, coastal surveillance, maritime domain awareness, public-private cyber capacity-building, and regional CERT or coast guard cooperation.
