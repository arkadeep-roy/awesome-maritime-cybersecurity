| Organization | Program type | Platform/source | Maritime relevance | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wärtsilä | Vulnerability Disclosure Program | [Wärtsilä report vulnerability](https://www.wartsila.com/contact/report-vulnerability), [Intigriti listing](https://app.intigriti.com/programs/wartsila/wartsila-vdp) | Marine and energy technology | Wärtsilä states it accepts good-faith vulnerability reports within scope and provides legal safe-harbor language. |
