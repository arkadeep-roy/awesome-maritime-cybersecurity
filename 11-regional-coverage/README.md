## Editorial standard

Regional entries should identify:

- relevant chokepoints, ports, sea lanes, or offshore infrastructure;
- regional regulators, port authorities, ISACs, research groups, or cyber capacity-building bodies;
- confirmed incidents and carefully labelled reported or suspected incidents;
- standards or national rules that materially affect maritime operators;
- source limitations where public information is thin.

Avoid publishing target lists, live infrastructure exposure lists, or operational scanning details.
