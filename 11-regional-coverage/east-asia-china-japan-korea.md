# East Asia: China, Japan, and Korea

Coverage includes China, Japan, South Korea, and adjacent East Asian maritime cyber concerns: port incidents, S-100/ECDIS research, shipping-line incidents, classification societies, shipbuilding, and smart-port ecosystems.

## Regional context

East Asia includes some of the world's largest shipbuilders, shipping companies, ports, electronics suppliers, navigation-system vendors, and hydrographic authorities. The region is central to container shipping, automotive export logistics, LNG flows, offshore energy, and maritime digitalization.

## Japan

### Port of Nagoya ransomware, 2023

The Port of Nagoya is a key Japanese port and an important automotive export hub. Public reporting says the July 2023 ransomware incident disrupted the port's container handling system and halted container operations for roughly two days. LockBit 3.0 was publicly associated with the attack.

Sources:

- [dragos.com](https://www.dragos.com/blog/ot-cybersecurity-breach-disrupts-operations-at-the-port-of-nagoya-japan)
- [cyber.gc.ca](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/cyber-threat-marine-transportation)
- [maritimecybersecurity.nl](https://maritimecybersecurity.nl/incident/9BEmB0xob7)

### Japanese technology/vendor context

Japan is relevant through:

- JRC and Furuno navigation/radar/AIS systems;
- ClassNK cyber-security guidance and IACS UR E26/E27 material;
- port automation and automotive export logistics;
- smart-port and terminal operating system dependencies;
- GNSS/AIS integrity in waters near Japan, Korea, China, Taiwan, and Russia.

## China

### COSCO cyber incident, 2018

COSCO Shipping Lines confirmed in July 2018 that a cyber attack affected internet connectivity within its offices in the Americas; reporting described email and network telephone disruption and containment by disconnecting affected regions.

Sources:

- [psabdp.com](https://psabdp.com/news/cosco-hit-by-cyber-attack)
- [cshub.com](https://www.cshub.com/attacks/news/incident-of-the-week-cosco-shipping-faces-ransomware-attack)

### Regulatory and geopolitical context

China coverage requires source-backed treatment. Relevant areas include:

- cyber incident reporting and critical information infrastructure rules affecting ports, shipping, logistics, and transport;
- port automation and smart-port platforms;
- shipping-line and terminal operator cyber dependencies;
- sanctions, dark-fleet, and AIS identity manipulation involving China-bound or China-linked trade routes;

## South Korea

### S-100, KHOA, and OpenS100

South Korea is relevant because of KHOA and S-100 implementation work. The OpenS100/KHOA S-100 Viewer ecosystem is relevant to CVE-2026-22208, where NVD describes remote code execution via an unrestricted Lua interpreter in portrayal catalogue handling.

Sources:

- [github.com](https://github.com/S-100ExpertTeam/khoa-s100-viewer)
