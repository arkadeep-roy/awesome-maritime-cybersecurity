# India Maritime Cybersecurity Ecosystem

India-specific maritime cybersecurity organizations, regulators, training programs, and research angles.

## Government and regulatory bodies

### Directorate General of Shipping

DG Shipping issued Engineering Circular No. 06 of 2017 on implementation of cybersecurity risk mitigation measures onboard Indian-flagged ships.

Sources:

- [dgma.gov.in](https://dgma.gov.in/engineering-wing/ew-circulars/engineering-ism-circulars)
- [irclass.org](https://www.irclass.org/technical-circulars/dg-shipping-circular-reg-implementation-of-cyber-security-risk-mitigation-measures-onboard-indian-flag-ships/)

### Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways

The ministry is the central ministry for ports, shipping, and waterways. It is relevant to port cybersecurity policy and Bureau of Port Security implementation.

Source:

- [shipmin.gov.in](https://shipmin.gov.in/)

### Bureau of Port Security

The Bureau of Port Security is being set up as a statutory body under the Merchant Shipping Act, 2025, with port and ship security oversight functions. Public government material explicitly mentions cybersecurity and port IT infrastructure protection.

Source:

- [pib.gov.in](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2206443)


## Classification and assurance

### Indian Register of Shipping / IRClass

IRClass has cyber-risk-management material and maritime cyber-safety guidelines.

Sources:

- [irclass.org](https://www.irclass.org/marine/cyber-risk-management/)
- [irclass.org](https://www.irclass.org/media/7145/irs-g-saf-02_guidelines-on-maritime-cyber-safety-rev1_march-2024.pdf)

## Training and capacity-building

### C3iHub, IIT Kanpur and Indian Ports Association

C3iHub conducted specialized maritime cybersecurity training for Indian Ports Association officials in December 2025.

Sources:

- [iitk.ac.in](https://www.iitk.ac.in/specialized-training-on-maritime-cybersecurity-for-ipa)
- [iitk.ac.in](https://www.iitk.ac.in/media-coverage-training-on-maritime-cybersecurity-for-ipa)

Topics reportedly covered:

- compliance and regulatory requirements
- social media threat profiling
- dark web analysis
- OSINT
- DFIR
- network security assessment
- cloud security
