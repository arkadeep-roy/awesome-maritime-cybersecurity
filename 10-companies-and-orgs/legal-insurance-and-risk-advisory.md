## Why this category matters

A maritime security research or vulnerability report usually explains:

- operational impact
- safety impact
- business interruption
- insurance relevance
- regulatory or class-assurance context
- third-party/vendor risk

Legal and insurance actors help translate technical findings into maritime business risk.
