# Maritime Cybersecurity Vendor Landscape

Vendor and advisory ecosystem map. Inclusion does not mean a company is vulnerable, in scope, endorsed, or open to testing.

## Classification and assurance

### DNV / DNV Cyber / CyberOwl

DNV acquired CyberOwl in September 2024. CyberOwl should therefore be tracked as a DNV-owned maritime cyber-risk monitoring and threat-management company, not as a fully independent vendor.

Sources:

- [dnv.com](https://www.dnv.com/news/dnv-and-cyberowl-join-forces-to-strengthen-cyber-security-in-shipping-as-tighter-regulations-come-into-force/)
- [cyberowl.io](https://cyberowl.io/)
- [dnv.com](https://www.dnv.com/cyber/industries/maritime/)

Classification:

```text
classification society / cyber advisory / onboard cyber-risk monitoring
```

### ABS Group

ABS Group provides maritime cybersecurity services and IACS E26/E27 compliance support. ABS states that E26/E27 regulations are mandatory for new ships contracted for construction on or after July 1, 2024.

Source:

- [abs-group.com](https://www.abs-group.com/Solutions/Cybersecurity/Maritime-Cybersecurity/Maritime-Cybersecurity-Compliance-to-Industry-Regulations/Cybersecurity-Compliance-to-IACS-E26-and-E27-Regulations/)

### Bureau Veritas

Bureau Veritas provides cyber services for newbuilds and suppliers and has material around IACS UR E26/E27.

Sources:

- [marine-offshore.bureauveritas.com](https://marine-offshore.bureauveritas.com/magazine/ensuring-cyber-resilience-meet-new-iacs-requirements)
- [marine-offshore.bureauveritas.com](https://marine-offshore.bureauveritas.com/marine-classification/cyber-services/newbuilds)

### IRClass

IRClass belongs in both the India file and the classification-society ecosystem.

Source:

- [irclass.org](https://www.irclass.org/marine/cyber-risk-management/)

## Maritime cyber vendors and services

### Cydome

Cydome describes itself as built-for-maritime cybersecurity for IT and OT, with centralized risk management and compliance acceleration.

Sources:

- [cydome.io](https://cydome.io/)
- [cydome.io](https://cydome.io/cydome-partners/cydome-security/)

### Naval Dome

Naval Dome is a maritime OT cyber-defense vendor focused on mission-critical onboard systems. Older public reporting says its software was positioned as Marine Dome, Port Dome, and Navy Dome for commercial vessels, ports, and naval applications.

Sources:

- [navaldome.com](https://navaldome.com/about.html)
- [oceannews.com](https://oceannews.com/news/defense/naval-dome-s-cyber-defense-software-for-port-and-naval-applications/)


### Marlink Cyber

Marlink is listed under connectivity and managed cyber services. It is relevant because satellite connectivity and managed vessel communications are part of maritime cyber risk.

Source:

- [marlink.com](https://marlink.com/services/cyber-security/)

### Dryad Global Cyber Voyager

Dryad Global offers maritime cyber services and is listed as a secondary vendor/advisory entry.

Source:

- [dryadglobal.com](https://www.dryadglobal.com/maritime-cyber-security)
