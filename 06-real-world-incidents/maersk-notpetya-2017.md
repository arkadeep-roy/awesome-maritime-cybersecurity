# Maersk NotPetya, 2017

## Summary

In June 2017, Maersk was heavily affected by NotPetya, a destructive malware outbreak that spread globally after compromising Ukrainian software supply chains. Maersk later estimated the cost at roughly USD 250-300 million according to widely cited reporting.

## Operational relevance

- Shows how enterprise IT compromise can disrupt shipping and port operations.
- Demonstrates the importance of recoverable backups and global incident response.
- Relevant for explaining business impact in maritime cyber reports.
