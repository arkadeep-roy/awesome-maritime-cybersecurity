# Ever Given, 2021: illustrative impact case

## Summary

The Ever Given Suez Canal blockage was not a cyber incident. It is included only as an impact-modeling example showing how a single vessel can disrupt a major maritime chokepoint.

## Why it belongs here

Security researchers often need to explain plausible business and operational impact. The Ever Given case helps frame why navigation, port, and chokepoint resilience matter, without claiming cyber causality.

## Boundary

Do not describe this as a cyberattack.
