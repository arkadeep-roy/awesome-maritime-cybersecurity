# Port of Nagoya ransomware, 2023

## Summary

In July 2023, Japan's Port of Nagoya experienced ransomware disruption affecting container operations. Multiple sources reported LockBit involvement and temporary suspension of container handling workflows.

## Lessons

- Terminal operating and container-handling systems are availability-critical.
- Ransomware can cause real-world logistics delays without directly touching vessel navigation.
- Port segmentation, recovery, and manual fallback are key defensive themes.
