# MSC Antonia grounding, 2025

## Summary

In May 2025, MSC Antonia ran aground near Jeddah. Several maritime and GNSS-focused sources reported suspected GPS spoofing or jamming as a contributing factor. 

## Lessons

- Navigation integrity depends on cross-checking GNSS with other sources.
- AIS-derived anomaly detection may support monitoring but does not prove attribution by itself.
