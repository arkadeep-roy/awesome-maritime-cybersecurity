# Jawaharlal Nehru Port Container Terminal cyberattack, 2022

## Summary

In February 2022, JNPCT in Mumbai reportedly suffered a cyberattack affecting its management information system. Reporting described disruption to terminal operations and manual processing.

## Lessons

- Port MIS and terminal workflows are availability-critical.
- Manual fallback plans are important for port resilience.
- Reports should distinguish confirmed ransomware from suspected ransomware where sources are not official.
