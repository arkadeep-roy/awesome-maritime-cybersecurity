# DNV ShipManager ransomware, 2023

## Summary

DNV confirmed a ransomware attack on ShipManager servers on 7 January 2023. DNV shut down affected servers in response. DNV stated that onboard offline functionality remained available and that the incident did not affect vessels' ability to operate.

## Impact

TechCrunch reported that DNV said 70 customers operating around 1,000 vessels were affected.

## Lessons

- Ship-management platforms are important operational dependencies.
- Offline vessel functionality and tested recovery procedures matter.
- Incident writeups should distinguish shore-side management impact from vessel-operability impact.
