# CMA CGM cyberattack, 2020

## Summary

CMA CGM confirmed in September 2020 that it was dealing with a cyberattack affecting peripheral servers and interrupted external access to applications to prevent malware spread. The company stated that maritime and port activities remained operational while IT access was restored.

## Lessons

- Public-facing customer and booking systems can be affected without stopping all maritime operations.
- Clear public incident updates reduce uncertainty for customers.
- External-access shutdown can be a containment measure.
